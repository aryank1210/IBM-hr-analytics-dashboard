# HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive HR analytics dashboard built in Power BI to analyze employee attrition, demographics, and job satisfaction trends across an organization.

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard provides HR stakeholders with a consolidated view of workforce data to help identify attrition drivers and support data-driven retention strategies.

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset (1,470 employee records)

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights

- **Employee Count:** 1,470 | **Average Age:** 37 | **Current Employees:** 1,233
- **Attrition Count:** 237 | **Attrition Rate:** 16%
- **Attrition by Department:** Sales and R&D show the highest attrition volumes
- **Attrition by Education Field:** Life Sciences and Medical fields show the highest attrition counts
- **Employee Distribution:** Breakdown by age band, gender, education level, and marital status
- **Job Satisfaction Ratings:** Cross-analyzed by job role (Healthcare Representative, Research Scientist, Sales Executive, etc.) on a 1–4 scale

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop** — data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive visualizations
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- Visuals used: KPI cards, pie/donut charts, clustered bar charts, matrix tables, and slicers
